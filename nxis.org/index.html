Questions worth asking

01

So… what exactly is Nexis?

Think of Nexis as a giant coffee shop for businesses — except instead of swapping stories, they swap APIs. It's where startups and organisations find each other, connect, and collaborate without spending months (and thousands) on integrations.

02

Why should my startup bother joining?

Because building alone is expensive and exhausting. Nexis lets you plug into a ready-made network of verified businesses — faster partnerships, instant credibility, and less technical chaos.

In short: fewer meetings, more doing.

03

Does joining Nexis make me magically compliant?

Nice try — but no. If you're a fintech in the UK, you still answer to the FCA. Nexis won't bend the law for you, it just makes following it way easier.

You still run your KYC, your audits, your local checks — we just cut out the integration headaches.

04

What's in it for me?

Access, revenue, and reach. You get a single interface to a much wider audience where you can publish, sell, and monetise your APIs — while tapping into countless others on-demand.

Pay for what you use, charge for what you offer, and connect with a community that actually believes collaboration is good for business and good for humanity.
