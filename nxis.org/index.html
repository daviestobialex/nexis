FAQ

What is Nexis?

Nexis stands for Network Exchange Integration System. It is an open standard protocol that allows businesses to easily discover each other, interact securely, and exchange services across borders.

How can Nexis help businesses?

Expanding a business across borders often means dealing with incompatible systems, fragmented APIs, and duplicated integrations.

With Nexis, businesses don’t need to reinvent every connection. Instead, they connect once to an open peer-to-peer standard—a shared protocol that lets them interoperate directly with other verified participants. Each peer brings its own APIs, compliance checks, or market services, but exposure happens through a common, lightweight library.

This means faster integration, less redundancy, and a network effect where every new participant strengthens the ecosystem for everyone.
